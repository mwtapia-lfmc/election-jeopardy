How to Play

  1. Pick a tile from the board to reveal a question.
  2. All teams race to buzz in — there's a 10-second window.
  3. The team that buzzes first gets four choices and a timer to lock in an answer.
  4. Correct: they win the points and the tile turns their team color.
  5. Wrong: they're locked out of that question, and the remaining teams can buzz in for it.
  6. If every team misses, the answer is revealed and the tile goes gray with an ✗.
  7. Play two rounds, then finish with Final Jeopardy — each team wagers before the last clue.

Highest score at the end wins.

About

Election Jeopardy is a political trivia game built for Zoom calls and live events — a fast, friendly way to test what your group knows about candidates, districts, and the road to the next election.
